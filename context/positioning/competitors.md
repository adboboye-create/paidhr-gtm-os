# PaidHR: Competitive Positioning

Who PaidHR competes with, where it wins and loses, and how to answer competitor objections. Read before writing competitive messaging or handling objections.
v1, 2026-11-12. From [competitors.md (research)](../docs/research/competitors.md) and other research files only. No win/loss data exists in research.

## Main competitors

- **SeamlessHR (Seamless Technologies)**: Targets mid-market and enterprise; office and shift-based teams. Pricing: Quote-based ("opaque" per reviewer). Strengths: ~2,000 customers, ~300,000 employees, 20 countries; >₦950bn processed 2025; >$20M raised; ERP integrations (SAP, Oracle, Sage, Microsoft); WhatsApp, EWA and payroll financing (Breeze), AI assistant (Samira). Weaknesses: Opaque pricing; enterprise weight. Kenya and Ghana: lists both as countries, with offices in Nairobi and Accra; payroll financing in Nigeria and Kenya. Source: cp:K1, K2, K4.
- **Workpay**: Targets growing African companies: 20 to 100 single-country; 100 to 1,000 multi-country. Pricing: TBD. Strengths: Nairobi HQ, so local in Kenya; 54 countries, 100+ currencies; EOR; "100% compliance" claim; ISO 27001; Visa-backed. Weaknesses: Performance Management "Coming soon". Source: cp:K7, K8.
- **Deel**: Targets global and remote teams. Pricing: TBD. Strengths: Global payroll and EOR; already serves African businesses. Weaknesses: Foreign platform; forex charges cited as a local pain. Source: cp:K5, K11.
- **Cadana**: Targets startups and remote-first teams. Pricing: TBD. Strengths: Cross-border to 90+ countries; modern interface; EWA. Weaknesses: HQ and depth on Nigerian remittance not confirmed. Source: cp:K10, K11.
- **NotchHR**: Targets Nigerian startups and SMEs. Pricing: Free. Strengths: 2,000+ businesses, 30,000 users. Weaknesses: Recency unverified. Source: cp:K11.
- **Salario**: Targets Nigerian SMEs. Pricing: Free payroll. Strengths: Tax compliance focus. Weaknesses: Recently launched; little detail. Source: cp:K11.
- **roPay**: Targets SMEs. Pricing: Per employee (₦200/employee for payroll, 2023, likely outdated). Strengths: Payroll-first. Weaknesses: Recency unverified. Source: cp:K11.
- **Sage Payroll**: Targets finance-led SMEs. Pricing: TBD. Strengths: Tight accounting integration. Weaknesses: n/a in research. Source: cp:K10.
- **PaySpace**: Targets high-growth, multi-country. Pricing: TBD. Strengths: Built for scaling across countries. Weaknesses: n/a in research. Source: cp:K10.
- **Bento Africa**: Targeted SMEs (was). Pricing: n/a. Strengths: n/a. Weaknesses: Collapsed Feb 2025 after tax and pension remittance probes; clients' staff unpaid. Source: cp:K9.
- **Oze**: Ghana. Named once in a competitor list; not researched. Source: cp:K6.
- **Excel / manual**: Targets everyone. Pricing: Free. Strengths: Familiar. Weaknesses: Errors, missed deductions, manual remittance; "didn't scale". Source: cp:K5, sp:V6, V7.

## Where PaidHR wins

- **Nigerian compliance depth**: Statutory remittance automated; ₦934M PAYE / ₦754M pension 2025, "zero defaults & delays"; third party: "designed for local compliance". Source: co:S1, co:S2, cp:K10.
- **Simplicity**: "Simple interface" (third party); FAMASI chose it over tools that "didn't seem very simple". Source: cp:K10, sp:V1.
- **Support**: "Strong support" (third party); Mono: "not the regular vendor account manager relationship"; Filmhouse, Sygnite praise. Source: cp:K10, sp:V4, V5, V10.
- **Fast, low-friction start**: No upfront onboarding fee; run payroll same day. Source: co:S7.
- **Cross-border payroll**: "Standout feature" (TechCabal). Source: cp:K6.
- **Disbursement speed**: "One of the fastest" (Filmhouse). Source: sp:V5.

## Where PaidHR loses

- **Scale and track record**: SeamlessHR ~2,000 customers vs PaidHR 400+ / 570+. Source: cp:K2, co:S1.
- **Funding / vendor size**: SeamlessHR >$20M, Workpay ~$10M vs PaidHR $2.3M to $2.97M. Source: cp:K2, K8, co:S5, S12.
- **Multi-country**: Workpay 54 countries plus EOR; no evidence of PaidHR entities outside Nigeria. Source: cp:K7, co: Gaps.
- **Kenya and Ghana local presence**: Workpay is Nairobi-based; SeamlessHR has offices in Nairobi and Accra; PaidHR has no evidence of local entities, customers or statutory support there. Source: cp:K1, K7, co: Gaps.
- **Enterprise integrations**: SeamlessHR lists SAP, Oracle, Sage, Microsoft. Source: cp:K1.
- **Price at the small end**: NotchHR and Salario are free. Source: cp:K11.
- **Feature uniqueness**: SeamlessHR matches WhatsApp, EWA, AI assistant. Source: cp:K1, K2.

## How to respond

- **"SeamlessHR is bigger"**: Respond with: built for your size: same-day start, no upfront fee, simple interface, named account manager. Source: co:S7, cp:K10, sp:V4.
- **"We need multiple countries" (Workpay / Deel)**: Respond with: if staff are in Nigeria, local compliance depth matters most; cross-border pay to 45+ currencies covers staff abroad. If they need EOR across Africa, not a fit. Source: co:S1, co:S8, cp:K7.
- **"A free tool works for us"**: Respond with: ask who files PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF and how they confirm it landed. Source: sp:V6, V7.
- **"Excel works"**: Respond with: Cleva: "As we grew it just didn't scale"; Go54 HR and CFO both re-checked every run. Source: sp:V7, V6.
- **"Can we trust a payroll vendor after Bento?"**: Respond with: remittance record "zero defaults & delays" 2025; self-generated payment receipts; no customer deposits held, licensed partners; ISO 27001. Source: co:S1, S2, S8, sp:V5.
