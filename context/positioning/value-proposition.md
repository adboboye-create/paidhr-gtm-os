# PaidHR: Value Proposition by Persona

Job to be done, pain, gain and proof for each persona and for employees. Read before writing copy aimed at a specific role.
v1, 2026-11-12. From `context/docs/research/` only. Personas: [personas.md](../icp/personas.md).

## Champion: HR lead

- **Job to be done**: Pay everyone correctly and on time, remit statutory deductions, and keep HR records, without living in spreadsheets.
- **Pain**: Excel errors and missed deductions (sp:V6); compliance as "a huge bone of contention" (sp:V4); payslip requests from "50 or more people every month" (sp:V6); a failing provider (sp:V5).
- **Gain**: Payroll "at the click of a button", run "on the day" (sp:V3); payslips self-served, "nobody even comes to me" (sp:V6); approvals that "catch the last-minute details" (sp:V5); "enough time now to focus on other core HR aspects" (sp:V6).
- **Proof**: Go54, Mono, MyCoverGenius, Filmhouse on camera (sp:V3 to V6); modules: custom approvals, off-cycle runs, ESS, HR workflows, Performance (co:S1).

## Economic buyer: CEO / founder

- **Job to be done**: Grow headcount without payroll eating founder time or creating tax and pension risk.
- **Pain**: Spreadsheet that "just didn't scale" (sp:V7); state-by-state PAYE (sp:V7); one hour per payroll run making payments by hand (sp:V1).
- **Gain**: That hour back, "rest assured that [PaidHR] has my people paid" (sp:V1); "it automatically computes what we need to pay them and it just works" (sp:V7); able to offer pension (sp:V7); start same day with no upfront onboarding fee (co:S7).
- **Proof**: FAMASI and Cleva CEOs on camera (sp:V1, V7); 400+ businesses and 25,000+ employees (co:S1, co:S2).

## Influencer: Finance lead / CFO

- **Job to be done**: Trust the payroll numbers and remittances without re-checking them.
- **Pain**: Separate HR and CFO checks on every payroll (sp:V6); payments not reflecting at statutory bodies (sp:V6).
- **Gain**: "We don't really do that [separate checks] because it's automated. It's clear. It's clean." (sp:V6); remittance receipts as evidence of payment (sp:V5).
- **Proof**: ₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension remitted in 2025, "zero defaults & delays" (co:S1, co:S2); ISO 27001, NDPA (co:S1); no customer deposits held, licensed partners (co:S8); payroll API, structured exports, ERP pushes (co:S1).
- **Gap**: No finance voice on camera (sp: Gaps).

## Employees (end users, not buyers)

- **Job to be done**: Get paid on time, see payslips and remittances, reach money before payday.
- **Gain**: Remittance "very reliable", email on every tax payment (sp:V8); EWA so staff "didn't need to go borrowing" (sp:V6); WhatsApp and USSD access with "No internet? No problem." (posts).
- **Proof**: PaidLife ₦650M disbursed, "zero non-performing loans" (2025) (co:S2); ₦150M EWA (2024) (co:S5).
