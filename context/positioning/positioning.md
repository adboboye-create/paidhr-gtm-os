# PaidHR: Positioning

PaidHR's category, target customer, alternatives, unique value, proof and positioning statement. Read before writing messaging or copy.
v1, 2026-11-12. From `context/docs/research/` only.

## Positioning elements

- **Category**: Full-suite HRMS (HR, payroll and statutory remittance in one platform) for African businesses, Nigeria-first. Source: co:S1, co:S2.
- **How others categorise it**: "A strong Nigerian-focused payroll solution designed for local compliance and ease of use". Source: cp:K10.
- **Target customer**: Companies with 20 to 500 employees in Nigeria (proven: monthly PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF), plus Kenya and Ghana as expansion markets from 2026-11-12. Source: co:S1, co:S6, cu:C4; [icp.md](../icp/icp.md) v3.
- **Kenya and Ghana**: no positioning yet. Every proof point below is Nigerian, and Workpay (Nairobi HQ) and SeamlessHR (offices in Nairobi and Accra) are local there (cp:K1, K7). Positioning for these markets waits on [icp.md](../icp/icp.md) open question 10.
- **Alternative today**: Excel plus manual or outsourced remittance; a payroll provider that is failing or slow; separate tools for HR, payroll and foreign staff. Source: sp:V5 to V8, posts, cp:K5, cp:K9.
- **Unique value**: Statutory remittance, payroll, HR, performance, time & attendance, cross-border pay and employee financial wellbeing (PaidLife, EWA) in one platform, with WhatsApp and USSD access for staff. Source: co:S1, co:S2, posts.
- **Proof**: ₦119.2bn salaries processed 2026 YTD; 400+ businesses, 25,000+ employees; ₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension remitted 2025 with "zero defaults & delays"; ISO 27001; customer stories from Mono, Go54, Filmhouse, Cleva, MyCoverGenius, FAMASI. Source: co:S1, co:S2; [case-studies.md](../docs/case-studies.md).
- **Caution on "unique"**: SeamlessHR now also offers WhatsApp self-service, EWA and payroll financing, and an AI assistant (cp:K1, cp:K2). PaidHR's difference is fit and focus (Nigerian compliance, simplicity, support, size), not exclusive features. See [competitors.md](competitors.md).

## Positioning statement

Nigeria only; a Kenya and Ghana statement waits on icp.md open question 10.

For Nigerian businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and manual remittance, PaidHR is the one HR platform that runs payroll, files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF, and manages people from hire to payslip. Unlike juggling separate tools or relying on a payroll provider you cannot see into, PaidHR automates statutory remittance (₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension remitted in 2025 with zero defaults and delays) and gives employees self-service and earned wage access, so HR leaders and founders get their time back and staff get paid right, on time.

Sources: co:S1, co:S2, sp:V1, sp:V6, sp:V7, posts.
