# PaidHR: Customer Stories Index

Every customer story found in `context/docs/research/`, with status and source. Read before naming a customer or using a quote in any asset.
v1, 2026-11-12. Long-form draft: [output/campaigns/customers.md](../../output/campaigns/customers.md).

## Status codes

- **OK**: usable after quote check and customer consent.
- **CHECK**: name or status must be confirmed first.
- **HOLD**: do not use.
- **EMP**: employee voice, not buyer.
- **Quotes**: Quotes come from YouTube auto-captions, lightly cleaned. Verify against the video before publishing.

## Customer stories

- **Go54 (formerly WhoGoHost)**: Size 50 to 70. Industry: web hosting, digital tools. Problem: Excel payroll errors; remittance not reflecting; HR and CFO double checks; 50+ manual payslips a month. Result: payroll in a few clicks; double check gone; self-serve payslips; EWA; metric-based reviews. Quote: "You don't really know what you're suffering until you move to something better." Status: CHECK (captions say "Co-creation Hub"; acquired by HostAfrica). Source: sp:V6.
- **Filmhouse Group**: Size TBD. Industry: cinema. Problem: previous payroll administrator "had internal issues". Result: "Seamless" after 6 to 7 months; fast disbursement; approvals; receipts; failed-payment alerts. Quote: "It's one of the fastest platforms where disbursement of salaries is concerned." Status: CHECK (speaker name). Source: sp:V5.
- **Mono**: Size TBD. Industry: fintech, open banking. Problem: compliance "a huge bone of contention". Result: staff paid on time and accurately; obligations met; strong account manager. Quote: "[PaidHR] has taken over some of the most critical processes that we have and has done it effectively." Status: CHECK (speaker spellings; acquired by Flutterwave). Source: sp:V4.
- **MyCoverGenius**: Size TBD. Industry: insurtech. Problem: payroll prep and admin. Result: payroll "on the day"; self-serve payslips; EWA used. Quote: "It's significantly easier to run payroll at the click of a button." Status: OK. Source: sp:V3.
- **Cleva**: Size almost 20. Industry: fintech, cross-border payments. Problem: Excel; multi-state PAYE via accounting firm walking to tax offices. Result: PAYE and pension automated (~4 months in); self-onboarding; pension now offered. Quote: "As we grew it just didn't scale." Status: OK. Source: sp:V7.
- **FAMASI**: Size TBD. Industry: health tech, pharmacy. Problem: manual payments and remittances; ~1 hour per run texting staff. Result: that hour saved; payroll reminders; easy remittance. Quote: "I have that one hour to be able to focus on other things." Status: CHECK (~2023, pre-rebrand). Source: sp:V1.
- **Sygnite Power & Energy**: Size TBD. Industry: solar, power engineering. Problem: onboarding new staff "overwhelming". Result: employees self-onboard; "pretty affordable"; accessible support. Quote: "With [PaidHR] this task has been made easier, which allows employees to self-onboard." Status: CHECK (~2023; surname). Source: sp:V10.
- **Real Fruit (name unverified)**: Size TBD. Industry: TBD. Problem: peculiar work challenges. Result: leave, loans, payroll; responsive to feedback. Quote: "[PaidHR] is very flexible and very responsive." Status: CHECK (company name; ~2023). Source: sp:V9.
- **Rise**: Size TBD. Industry: fintech, wealth. Problem: previous platform: pension 3 to 4 months late, no tax notifications. Result: remittance "complete", "accurate", "consistent"; email on each payment. Quote: "The remittance is very reliable and I'm very much aware of what's going on." Status: EMP. Source: sp:V8.
- **Heroshe**: Size TBD. Industry: cross-border shipping. Problem: statutory remittance took "two weeks or more"; tax office calls. Result: fast remittance with receipts; self-serve payslips. Quote: "I leave the software to do the operational tasks and I do the strategic tasks." Status: HOLD (company may have failed). Source: sp:V2.
- **Transport Services Ltd (TSL)**: Size 2,000. Industry: logistics. Problem: n/a. Result: first customer; pushed PaidHR to enterprise-ready. Quote: none. Status: CHECK (search summary only). Source: cu:C1.
- **M&W Nuts**: Size TBD. Industry: TBD. Problem: TBD. Result: TBD. Quote: video on LinkedIn, not transcribed. Status: CHECK (no transcript). Source: cu:C2.
- **GetEquity**: Size TBD. Industry: fintech, investment. Problem: cost of living. Result: co-founder uses EWA monthly. Quote: "Is there any month I don't use it since it launched?" Status: HOLD (PaidHR not named in captions). Source: sp:V11.
- **KundaKids**: Size TBD. Industry: edtech. Problem: needed a salary advance. Result: used salary advance via app. Quote: "My HR said that we can use this on the app... I tried it and it worked." Status: HOLD (PaidHR not named in captions). Source: sp:V12.
