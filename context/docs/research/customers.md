Collected 2026-11-11 from public sources. Raw notes, not conclusions. Source tags refer to the list at the bottom and to [company.md](company.md).

**Update 2026-11-11 (later): customer video testimonials found.** 12 customer and user videos were transcribed from paidhr.com/customers and PaidHR's YouTube channel: FAMASI, Heroshe, MyCoverGenius, Mono, Filmhouse Group, Go54, Cleva, Rise, "Real Fruit", Sygnite Power, GetEquity, KundaKids. **Original finding (earlier on 2026-11-11): public customer evidence is thin.** paidhr.com/customers is blocked to automated fetches (Cloudflare) and the version that loaded showed no named logos or quotes. No PaidHR listing was found on G2, Capterra, GetApp or Trustpilot. No case studies were indexed by search. The best leads are the LinkedIn series "The People Who Get Paid" and the first-customer story below. A manual look at paidhr.com/customers in a browser is the next step.

## Case studies and testimonials

- **Customer quotes: none found in text form.** The only customer testimonial content located is the M&W Nuts video in "The People Who Get Paid" series [C2].
- Investor quote (not a customer): Amee Parbhoo, Managing Partner, Accion Venture Lab: "PaidHR's integrated platform is tackling deep-rooted structural challenges faced by small businesses" [C4]
- Founder quote on customers: Lekan Omotosho, CTO: "Our clients trust us with their most sensitive workforce data and payroll processes." [C4]
- Product team on customer-led roadmap: Seye Bandele: "some of our users have requested this feature" (cross-border payroll). Ore Badmus: "We are only adding solutions our users have requested to our product." [C8]
- App store user reviews (third-party aggregation, as of 11 May 2026): 4.1 on Google Play from 49 reviews; 5.0 on App Store from 2 reviews. Example review: "Highly impressive and user-friendly platform." [C9]
