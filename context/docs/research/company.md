# PaidHR: Company (raw research)

Collected 2026-11-11 from public sources. Raw notes, not conclusions. Every fact has a source tag; see Sources at the bottom. Where sources disagree, all versions are listed. Em dashes inside quotes have been replaced with commas or colons (CLAUDE.md rule); wording is otherwise verbatim.

## What they sell

- Homepage headline: "One compliant HR Software for the entire employee lifecycle." [S1]
- LinkedIn tagline: "One platform HRMS for fast-growing African enterprises and employee financial wellness." [S2]
- LinkedIn About: "PaidHR is the full-suite HRMS that unifies HR, payroll, time & attendance, performance and financial wellness in one platform, giving African enterprises total control of people operations, and their employees real financial wellbeing." [S2]
- LinkedIn "Who we serve": "For fast-growing and enterprise organizations across Africa that need to run people operations without stitching together disconnected tools." [S2]
- LinkedIn differentiation line: "Unlike fragmented point tools and legacy systems, PaidHR adapts natively to existing workflows and gives employees self-service visibility and control over their work and their money." [S2]
- CEO framing: "We are building HR management for the African context" [S4][S5]. Also: "We're primarily an HR tech company" [S5].
- Revenue model: cross-border payroll earns transaction fees [S8]. PaidHR does not hold customer deposits; it partners with licensed financial institutions [S8].

## Modules and products

From the homepage [S1]:
| Module | Homepage description |
|---|---|
| Statutory Remittance | Automated PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF filings |
| Cross-Border Payroll | Pay teams in 45+ currencies |
| Payroll | Custom approvals, split disbursement, off-cycle runs |
| Hiring | Candidate pipeline from job post to signed offer (launched as "PaidHiring" ATS, Sept 2024 [S7]) |
| HR Tools & Workflows | Employee records, requests, approvals, feedback |
| Time & Attendance | Geo-referenced clock-ins, shifts, overtime flowing into payroll |
| Employee Benefits | Health cover, perks, support |
| Performance Management | OKR cascades and review cycles; Balanced Scorecard promoted on LinkedIn Nov 2026 [S3] |

Employee-side and newer products:
- **PaidLife**: employee money app. Earned wage access, loans, bill payments, payslips and a wallet [S2]. Promoted on LinkedIn from roughly Jul 2026 [S3].
- **Earned Wage Access** launched October 2023 [S7]. ₦150M disbursed via EWA in 2024 [S5].
- **Employee wallet** launched April 2025 [S5]. Multi-currency wallet lets staff convert earnings [S8].
- **Loan Marketplace** and mobile app for payslips, schedules, leave [S1].
- **WhatsApp and USSD access** for employees (payslips, leave, EWA without the app or internet), launched roughly Oct 2026 [S3].
- **PaidGuide**: AI assistant for HR teams to navigate the platform, announced roughly 10 Nov 2026 [S3].
- **PaidRight**: salary benchmarking report from payroll data of 25,000+ workers and 400+ employers (July 2026 snapshot). Median monthly gross salary ₦217,000 ($163) [S3][S9].
- **Vuzo**: a third-party LinkedIn post (not from PaidHR) says PaidHR launched "Vuzo", unbundling its payroll engine into embeddable infrastructure for fintechs, HR platforms, neobanks and vertical SaaS [S3]. Not confirmed by any PaidHR-owned source found. Homepage separately mentions a payroll API, structured exports, and ERP and finance-system pushes [S1], plus consultants and agencies running payroll businesses on PaidHR [S1].
- **Partnership**: YALO (employee housing benefits and rental financing), announced on LinkedIn roughly Sept 2026 [S3].
- Compliance claims: ISO 27001 certified, NDPA registered [S1][S2].

## Pricing model

- No public prices. Pricing page lists three plans with CTAs to book a demo or contact sales [S6]:
  - **Standard**: "20 - 100" employees. Includes HRIS, payroll management, statutory remittance, Earned Wage Access, employee financial benefits, approval workflow, employee wallet, ESS, surveys, document management, disciplinary management, request management, reports.
  - **Enterprise**: "100+" employees. "Everything in Standard +" "Dedicated support".
  - **Custom**: "PaidHR suite and more", Contact Sales.
- Pricing page line: "Our flexible pricing allows you to only pay for what you need." [S6]
- Sept 2024: removed the upfront onboarding fee; "new customers can now sign up, onboard, and begin running payroll all on the same day." (Nancy-Olive Tamuno, Product Marketing Specialist) [S7]
- Per-employee price: unknown. TBD.

## Markets

- HQ: 11 Providence Street, Lekki Phase, Lagos, Nigeria [S10]. LinkedIn HQ: Lekki, Lagos [S2].
- Markets named in funding coverage: Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa [S4][S5].
- Product is Nigeria-first: statutory remittance is built around Nigerian PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF [S1]. A third-party listing notes support for Nigerian tax law changes effective 1 January 2026 [S13].
- Cross-border payroll currencies: "45+" [S1], "49" [S2][S8]. Sources differ.
