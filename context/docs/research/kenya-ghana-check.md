# Kenya and Ghana check (icp.md open question 10)

What public sources say about PaidHR in Kenya and Ghana: local payroll, statutory remittance and named customers. Read before writing any Kenya or Ghana copy or answering icp.md open question 10.
2026-11-12. Free web search and page fetches only; no treg spend. Nothing here is confirmed by PaidHR.

## Answer so far

- **Q10a, local payroll in Kenya and Ghana**: PARTIAL. PaidHR's CEO says it already supports companies in Kenya, South Africa and Ghana (KG2), and Accion says 250+ businesses across Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa use it, some running multi-country payroll (KG1). The same article frames the regional growth as cross-border payroll "without establishing offices in those jurisdictions" (KG2). Unknown whether a company based in Kenya or Ghana runs its own local payroll on PaidHR, or only pays staff there from a Nigerian payroll.
- **Q10b, statutory deductions**:
  - **Nigeria**: confirmed in PaidHR's help centre and product pages: PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF, ITF, including multi-state PAYE (KG4, KG5, KG6).
  - **Kenya**: PARTIAL. PaidHR's own blog says it is "built around" "Kenyan statutory deductions" (KG3), and Accion says remittance automation is "especially valuable in markets like Nigeria and Kenya" (KG1). No PaidHR page or help article names a Kenyan deduction (PAYE to KRA, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, NITA), and the statutory remittance product page names no country outside Nigeria (KG5).
  - **Ghana**: NO EVIDENCE. Ghana appears only in the CEO quote (KG2) and in the blog's "Regional focus: Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana" (KG3). No source mentions SSNIT, Tier 2 or GRA PAYE.
- **Q10c, a nameable customer**: NONE FOUND. No Kenyan or Ghanaian customer is named in any source.

## Local context (for later copy, third-party, not PaidHR claims)

- **Kenya 2026 employer deductions**: PAYE 10 to 35% to KRA; NSSF 6% employer and 6% employee up to KES 108,000 pensionable pay from Feb 2026; SHIF 2.75% employee; Affordable Housing Levy 1.5% employer and 1.5% employee; NITA KES 50 per employee per month; most due by the 9th of the next month. Source: KL1, KL2.
- **Ghana 2026 employer deductions**: SSNIT Tier 1 13.5% (employer 13%, employee 5.5%, of which 2.5% goes to NHIA), Tier 2 5% to a private trustee; SSNIT due within 14 days after month end, 3% monthly penalty if late; PAYE to GRA due the 15th of the next month; maximum insurable earnings GH₵69,000 from 1 Jan 2026; new PAYE bands (Act 1178) from 1 Sep 2026. Source: GL1, GL2. Some figures differ between sources; verify before use.
- **Competitors already publishing local guides**: SeamlessHR has Kenya and Ghana payroll guides; PayrollMaster Africa lists KRA and GRA filings. Source: KL3, GL1, KL4.
