## SeamlessHR (now Seamless Technologies)

- Homepage headline: "AI-powered HR Management Platform." Sub: "Streamline HR with AI-powered software built for office-based and shift-based teams" [K1].
- Calls itself "Africa's top enterprise-grade HR and payroll SaaS company" [K2].
- Jul 2026 rebrand to Seamless Technologies to expand into finance and AI. Products: Samira AI assistant; Breeze (embedded finance: payroll financing, EWA, employee financial services); SeamlessProcure (procurement) [K2][K3].
- Own blog: organisations need more than HR software [K2].
- Modules: HRMS, Payroll, Performance, Recruitment, Time Management, Employee Benefits; shift-based: Attendance, Mobile Self-Service, Communications. ERP integrations: Microsoft, Sage, SAP, Oracle. Payroll financing in Nigeria and Kenya only. WhatsApp leave and payslip requests [K1].
- Countries listed: Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa; offices Lagos, Nairobi, Accra [K1].
- Scale: nearly 2,000 business customers, payroll data for ~300,000 employees, 20 African countries [K2]. Processed over ₦950bn (> $600M) in salaries in 2025 [K4].
- Funding: $10M Series A (TLcom, Jan 2022); $9M Series A extension (Jan 2025); "over $20 million" raised [K2][K6].
- Case study on homepage: Afya Care, "50%" less time running payroll [K1].
- Founded 2017 or 2018 (sources differ); CEO Dr Emmanuel Okeleji [K2][K6].
- Reviewer view: fits mid-market and enterprise; weakness is quote-based, opaque pricing [K2].
- Explored acquiring PaidHR informally in Jan 2025 [K6].
- Feature overlap with PaidHR's 2026 launches: AI assistant (Samira vs PaidGuide), WhatsApp self-service, EWA and payroll financing (Breeze vs PaidLife).

## Workpay

- Homepage headline: "The EOR and Payroll Solution for Africa." Sub: "Payroll, EOR, and Outsourced Payroll Solutions for growing companies in Africa" [K7].
- Claims: "Run self-serve payroll across all 54 countries across Africa from one dashboard"; "Disburse salaries in more than 100 local currencies"; "Pay employees on time, every time"; "100% compliance"; ISO 27001 [K7].
- Modules: EOR, Outsourced Payroll, Payroll and Self Service HR, Financial Services, Expense Claims, Performance Management ("Coming soon"); add-ons Time and Attendance, Perks & Benefits, Payouts, Leave [K7].
- Segments listed: Startups, Banks, MFIs, Healthcare, Transport & Logistics [K7].
- Customers: "1,000+ Companies" (homepage also says "over 400" elsewhere) [K7]; >1,000 customers across 20 countries, >$200M annual payroll value (Aug 2024) [K8].
- Two customer types: 20-100 employees in one jurisdiction; 100-1,000 across regions [K8].
- Funding: YC W20; $2.1M seed (2020); $2.7M (Feb 2023); $5M Series A led by Norrsken22 with Visa (Aug 2024); "nearly $10 million" total [K8].
- Founders: Paul Kimani (CEO), Jackson Kibigo [K8].

## Bento Africa (collapsed)

- Was: Lagos payroll and HR startup (2019), payroll plus embedded financial services, available in Kenya, Ghana, Rwanda [K6][K9].
- Jan to Feb 2025: investigated by LIRS and EFCC over alleged failure to remit taxes and pensions; CEO Ebun Okubanjo resigned; tech team dismissed; temporary shutdown, customers told not to fund payroll accounts; some clients' staff not paid in first week of Feb 2025 [K9].
- Reported clients that left: Moniepoint, Paystack, Kobo360 [K9].
- Listed among 2025 startup failures by WeeTracker [K9].
- Note: Innovation Village's May 2026 "Top 7" still lists Bento as an option [K10]; that listing appears stale.
- Relevance: PaidHR publicly claims "zero defaults and zero delays" on 2025 PAYE and pension remittance [company.md S2].

## Cadana

- Africa-focused payroll and benefits; cross-border payroll to 90+ countries; suits companies in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa with staff abroad [K11].
- Innovation Village (May 2026): best for "Startups and remote-first teams"; "Modern interface, supports global teams"; EWA and contractor payments [K10].

## NotchHR

- "A free, easy-to-use HR system built in Nigeria for startups and SMEs"; reports 2,000+ businesses and 30,000 users in Nigeria [K11]. Recency not verified.

## roPay

- Lagos, founded 2020; payroll for SMEs then HR; per-employee pricing, N200/employee for payroll (2023, from Motherboard, itself an HR vendor; likely outdated) [K11].

## BizEdge, Motherboard, Xceed365HR, SystemSpecs

- BizEdge and Motherboard named as Nigerian HR-tech players competing for share (Jan 2025) [K6]. BizEdge site returned no content when fetched.
- Xceed365HR CEO: many Nigerian companies still run payroll on foreign software that doesn't fit local conditions [K11].
- SystemSpecs (HumanManager) named as a local option avoiding forex charges of foreign platforms (2022) [K11]. No current detail found.

## Salario

- Launched a free payroll product for Nigerian SMEs focused on tax compliance [K11]. Launch date not captured.

## Sage Payroll, PaySpace (from Innovation Village, May 2026) [K10]

- Sage: "a trusted option", payroll "tightly integrated with accounting systems"; best for "Finance-led SMEs".
- PaySpace: "designed for companies scaling across multiple countries"; best for "High-growth SMEs and multi-country operations".

## Global players

- Deel: already serves businesses in Africa [K5]; global payroll and EOR for remote teams [K11].
- Also named in SeamlessHR's competitive set: SAP, Zoho [K6]. CB Insights lists Zelt and OnPay among PaidHR's competitors [company.md S12].
- Other names seen once: FaidiHR, ElevateHR, Workskuad, PesaChoice (CB Insights, Workpay competitors); Oze (Ghana), FloatPays (South Africa) [K6].

## How third parties position PaidHR against this set

- Innovation Village (May 2026): PaidHR is "a strong Nigerian-focused payroll solution designed for local compliance and ease of use"; best for "Nigerian SMEs prioritising compliance"; strengths "Local expertise, strong support, simple interface" [K10].
- TechCabal (Jan 2025): PaidHR's cross-border payroll "has been a standout feature" and was a key reason for SeamlessHR's interest [K6].
