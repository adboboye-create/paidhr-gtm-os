# PaidHR: Customer video sources for social proof (raw)

Collected 2026-11-11. Source bank for building social proof documents. Raw research, not finished copy.

## How this was pulled

1. **paidhr.com/customers** blocks plain fetches (Cloudflare). Rendered it in a real browser via treg (litescrape). The "Business Spotlights / Growing businesses like yours #GETPaid" section embeds 2 unique YouTube videos (FAMASI, Heroshe; FAMASI is embedded twice). The page menu also embeds a product video (Cross Border).
2. Listed every video on PaidHR's YouTube channel (**@GetPaidHR**, 68 videos across 3 pages) and picked every customer or user testimonial: 9 more.
3. Pulled YouTube captions for all 14 videos via treg (scrapecreators) and checked titles via YouTube oEmbed.
4. Verified company and speaker names with web search where the captions garbled them.

Full unedited transcripts: [transcripts/](transcripts/). One file per video.

## Read before using any quote

- **Transcripts are YouTube auto-captions, not human transcriptions.** They mishear brand names ("paid", "PayDay HR", "Pade" all mean PaidHR or its old name), people's names and numbers. Quotes below are lightly cleaned: edits are in [square brackets], filler words removed with "...". **Watch the timestamp in the video and confirm wording before publishing.**
- **Names marked "unverified" must be confirmed** with PaidHR marketing or the customer before use.
- **Permission:** these videos are public on PaidHR's channel, but confirm each customer is still a customer and still happy to be quoted (see Risks).
- Per CLAUDE.md: no invented numbers. The only figures here are what speakers said on camera.
