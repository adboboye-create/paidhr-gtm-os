# PaidHR: Buyer Personas

The three buying roles (HR lead, CEO / founder, finance lead) with titles, pains in their own words, triggers and objections. Read before targeting a role or writing copy for one.
v1, 2026-11-12. From `context/docs/research/` only. Quotes are from customer videos (auto-captions, lightly cleaned; verify before publishing, see sp: "Read before using any quote").
**"Measured on"** = what each persona said they care about on camera. No research source states formal KPIs; those are open questions.

## 1. Champion: HR lead

- **Titles**: Head of HR; Head of People; Head of People and Culture; Lead HR Operations; Senior People Associate; Head of Human Resources. Source: sp:V3, V4, V5, V6, V9.
- **Measured on**: Staff paid on time and accurately; statutory obligations met; no salary-delay complaints; time for "core HR" work. Source: sp:V4, V5, V6.
- **Pain 1, manual payroll errors**: Quote: "We were using the manual computation... the Excel sheet, so... there's a chance of inputting a wrong figure." Source: sp:V6.
- **Pain 2, statutory compliance risk**: Quote: Compliance and statutory obligations were "a huge bone of contention for us." Source: sp:V4.
- **Pain 3, admin crowding out HR work**: Quote: "Imagine giving payslips of about 50 or more people every month." / "It eradicated a lot of administrative tasks, which I personally don't like to do." Source: sp:V6, sp:V3.
- **What makes them start looking**: Current provider fails ("had internal issues"); CEO asks them to evaluate a tool; growth breaks processes. Source: sp:V5, V6, V3.
- **Decided by**: Demo; approval workflows; compliance depth; visible support team. Source: sp:V4, V5, V6.
- **Objections in research**: Budget: needed a provider "that could fit within our budget". Source: sp:V5.
- **Objections not in research**: Migration effort, employee adoption: open question. Source: n/a.

## 2. Economic buyer: CEO / founder

In small companies the founder runs payroll; in larger ones the CEO initiates and approves.

- **Titles**: CEO; Co-founder. Source: sp:V1, V7, V6.
- **PaidHR targets them**: Moonshot 2026: "connecting with founders, HR leaders, and business operators". Source: posts.
- **Measured on**: Time to run the company; staying compliant as headcount grows; looking after staff (pension). Source: sp:V1, V7.
- **Pain 1, spreadsheet does not scale**: Quote: "I had this gigantic Excel spreadsheet... As we grew it just didn't scale." Source: sp:V7.
- **Pain 2, multi-state remittance is complex**: Quote: "The remittance to the various institutions across the country, it's state by state, is also complex." Source: sp:V7.
- **Pain 3, founder time lost to payroll**: Quote: "I had to [earmark] about one hour of the day whenever I [had] to run payroll just to manually make payments." Source: sp:V1.
- **What makes them start looking**: Hiring past a handful of people; contractors moving to full-time staff; wanting to offer pension. Source: sp:V7.
- **Decided by**: Simplicity ("looked very simple"); automated tax and pension; trust in a reachable team or founder. Source: sp:V1, V7.
- **Objections in research**: Other tools "didn't seem very simple to use"; worry about getting support "if there were to be any issue". Source: sp:V1, V7.
- **Context objection**: Payroll vendor trust after Bento Africa collapse (clients' staff unpaid). Source: cp:K9.
- **Pricing**: Not public; demo or contact sales only. Source: co:S6.

## 3. Influencer: Finance lead / CFO

- **Thin evidence**: No finance leader speaks in any research source (sp: Gaps). Below is all the research supports.
- **Titles**: CFO. Source: sp:V6.
- **Role seen**: Ran a separate check on every payroll alongside HR. Source: sp:V6.
- **Measured on**: Not stated in research: open question. Source: n/a.
- **Pain, double-checking payroll (no finance voice on camera)**: Closest quote: "I have to do my checks, the CFO has to do his check too... Both of us have to do separate checks." (Head of HR). Source: sp:V6.
- **Pain, payments not landing at statutory bodies**: Closest quote: "We may pay, it may not reflect at the statutory bodies." (Head of HR). Source: sp:V6.
- **Pain, cash-flow gaps around payroll**: Closest quote: PaidHR copy: "keep payroll on track during cash-flow gaps" (PaidHR's words, not a customer's). Source: posts.
- **What makes them start looking**: Not in research: open question. Source: n/a.
- **Proof available for them**: ₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension remitted in 2025, "zero defaults & delays"; ISO 27001; NDPA registered; no customer deposits held, licensed partners; payroll API and ERP pushes. Source: co:S1, co:S2, co:S8.
- **Objections**: Not in research: open question. Source: n/a.
