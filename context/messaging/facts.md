Kenya and Ghana became target markets on 2026-11-12 ([icp.md](../icp/icp.md) v3), but no claim here is approved for copy sent to Kenyan or Ghanaian prospects except company-level facts that are not about Nigeria (ISO 27001, no customer deposits held, cross-border pay in 45+ currencies, seed funding). Kenyan and Ghanaian claims get added here only after icp.md open question 10 is answered. Public evidence so far (CEO quote, Accion, PaidHR blog) is in [kenya-ghana-check.md](../docs/research/kenya-ghana-check.md); none of it is cleared for copy until PaidHR confirms.

- **Salaries processed**: ₦47.2bn. Date / scope: 2025. Source: co:S2.
- **Businesses / employees**: 400+ businesses, 25,000+ employees. Date / scope: 2026. Source: co:S1, S2.
- **PAYE / pension remitted**: ₦934M PAYE, ₦754M pension, "zero defaults & delays". Date / scope: 2025. Source: co:S1, S2.
- **PaidLife disbursed**: ₦650M, "zero non-performing loans". Date / scope: 2025. Source: co:S2.
- **Employer-backed loans**: ₦8bn. Date / scope: 2025. Source: co:S2.
- **EWA disbursed**: ₦150M. Date / scope: 2024. Source: co:S5.
- **Cross-border payments**: $2M+. Date / scope: since Oct 2024. Source: co:S1.
- **Cross-border currencies**: 45+ (homepage). Date / scope: 2026. Source: co:S1.
- **Security**: ISO 27001 certified; NDPA registered. Date / scope: 2026. Source: co:S1, S2.
- **Money handling**: Does not hold customer deposits; partners with licensed financial institutions. Date / scope: Oct 2024. Source: co:S8.
- **Onboarding**: No upfront onboarding fee; sign up, onboard and run payroll same day. Date / scope: Sept 2024. Source: co:S7.
- **What the platform does (payroll)**: Calculates and remits PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF each cycle with receipts; payroll approval workflows; staff self-onboard and get payslips themselves. Date / scope: 2026. Source: co:S1; sp:V3, V5, V10.
- **What the platform does (time and attendance)**: Geo-referenced clock-ins, shifts and overtime flow into payroll. Date / scope: 2026 (homepage module list). Source: co:S1.
- **What the platform does (disbursement)**: HR can self-generate a payment receipt for any salary payment; failed payments trigger an alert and can be re-sent. Date / scope: customer description, ~Dec 2025. Source: sp:V5.
- **Nigeria Tax Act (context, not a PaidHR claim)**: In force 1 January 2026, so 2026 is the first year under it; annual PAYE returns due 31 January. Never claim PaidHR files the annual return (unknown). Date / scope: 2026. Source: ss:E1.
- **Seed funding**: $1.8M led by Accion Venture Lab. Date / scope: Jun 2025. Source: co:S4, S5.
- **Salary data**: PaidRight: median monthly gross ₦217,000 across 25,000+ workers, 400+ employers. Date / scope: Jul 2026 snapshot. Source: co:S3, S9.
- **Third-party view**: "A strong Nigerian-focused payroll solution designed for local compliance and ease of use". Date / scope: May 2026. Source: cp:K10.
- **Customer figures (on camera only)**: FAMASI ~1 hour saved per payroll run; Go54 50 to 70 staff, ~50+ manual payslips before; Cleva almost 20 staff. Date / scope: see sp: section 4. Source: sp:V1, V6, V7.
