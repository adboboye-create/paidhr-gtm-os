# PaidHR: Messaging

Core message, pillars, persona angles and words to use or avoid. Read before writing any copy.
v1, 2026-11-12. From `context/docs/research/` only. Use with [facts.md](facts.md) (what we may claim) and [brand.md](../brand-kit/brand.md) (how we say it).

## Core message

**One platform to pay your people right, stay compliant and run HR, so you can stop juggling spreadsheets and get back to building your business.**

Built from PaidHR's own lines: "One compliant HR Software for the entire employee lifecycle" (co:S1); "Payroll shouldn't require multiple spreadsheets, endless approvals and constant double-checking" (posts); "Work Made Simple, Lives Made Better" (posts).

## Pillars

1. **Compliance, handled**: PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF calculated and remitted every cycle, with receipts. Proof: ₦934M PAYE, ₦754M pension remitted 2025, "zero defaults & delays"; Mono: compliance focus "takes a chunk of our stress away"; Cleva: state-by-state remittance now automated; Rise employee: "always complete... accurate and consistent". Source: co:S1, S2; sp:V4, V7, V8.
2. **Payroll in a few clicks**: Accurate payroll with approvals, run on the day, paid fast. Proof: ₦119.2bn processed 2026 YTD; Go54: "with a few clicks, salaries get paid", HR and CFO double check gone; MyCoverGenius: "today's 28th. Let's go."; Filmhouse: "one of the fastest" disbursements, approvals "catch the last-minute details". Source: co:S1, S2; sp:V3, V5, V6.
3. **One platform, whole lifecycle**: Hiring, records, leave, attendance, performance, payroll in one place. Proof: Modules: Hiring, HR Tools, Time & Attendance, Performance, Payroll, Benefits; Go54: performance "no longer subjective"; Cleva: self-onboarding and HR records off Excel; Sygnite: "employees self-onboard". Source: co:S1; sp:V6, V7, V10.
4. **Better for employees**: Self-service payslips, earned wage access and loans, on app, WhatsApp or USSD. Proof: PaidLife ₦650M disbursed, "zero non-performing loans" (2025); Go54: staff "didn't need to go borrowing"; "No internet? No problem." Source: co:S2; sp:V6; posts.

## Angle by persona

- **HR lead (champion)**: Lead pillar 1 then 2. Angle: get your time back for real HR work. Hook (customer words): "I have enough time now to focus on other core HR aspects". Source: sp:V6.
- **CEO / founder (economic buyer)**: Lead pillar 2 then 1. Angle: grow without payroll slowing you down or creating tax risk. Hook (customer words): "As we grew it just didn't scale". Source: sp:V7.
- **Finance lead / CFO (influencer)**: Lead pillar 1. Angle: numbers and remittances you do not need to re-check. Hook (customer words): "It's automated. It's clear. It's clean." Source: sp:V6.
- **Employees (adoption)**: Lead pillar 4. Angle: your pay, payslips and remittances, visible and on time. Hook (customer words): "I always get email notifications when my tax payment is made". Source: sp:V8.

## Words to use

- **simple, simpler, easier**: PaidHR's brand line and customers' top word. Source: posts; sp:V1, V3.
- **one platform**: PaidHR's main LinkedIn message. Source: co:S2, posts.
- **compliant, on time, every cycle**: Core remittance promise. Source: co:S1, posts.
- **a few clicks, at the click of a button**: Customers' own phrase. Source: sp:V3, V6.
- **relief, peace of mind**: Customers' feeling; Go54 video title. Source: sp:V6, V7.
- **PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF**: Buyers' own terms; specific beats "taxes". Source: co:S1, posts.
- **people operations, employees, your people**: PaidHR's own vocabulary. Source: co:S2, posts.

## Avoid

- **Em dashes**: House rule. Source: CLAUDE.md.
- **Cost savings, ROI, % time saved**: No customer stated them. Source: sp: section 4.
- **"Zero penalties" for a named customer**: No customer said it; the "zero defaults" claim is PaidHR's own 2025 figure. Source: sp:V6, V7; co:S2.
- **"Only platform with WhatsApp / EWA / AI assistant"**: SeamlessHR has all three. Source: cp:K1, K2.
- **Pan-African or Kenya / Ghana / South Africa presence**: No evidence of entities or customers there. Kenya and Ghana are target markets (icp.md v3), but pillar 1 names Nigerian deductions and every proof point is Nigerian, so none of the pillars may go to Kenyan or Ghanaian prospects unchanged. Source: co: Gaps; facts.md country scope.
- **Vuzo or embedded payroll**: Only a third-party source. Source: co:S3.
- **"Payroll provider" only**: PaidHR positions as full HRMS: "beyond payroll". Source: posts.
