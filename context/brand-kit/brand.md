# PaidHR: Brand Voice

PaidHR's tone of voice, key phrases and house rules. Read before producing any customer-facing asset.
v1, 2026-11-12. From PaidHR's own copy only: 48 LinkedIn posts Jul to Nov 2026 (`posts` = [raw JSON](../docs/research/raw/linkedin-posts-getpaidhr-2026-11-11.json)), homepage and LinkedIn page ([company.md](../docs/research/company.md) S1, S2, S6).

## Tone of voice

- **Problem first**: Opens with a relatable pain, then the fix, then the CTA. Example: "One task is manageable. Five systems? That's where the headache starts."
- **Plain and short**: Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon beyond HR terms. Example: "Performance management shouldn't live in your memory."
- **Triplets**: Rhythmic lists of three, often fragments. Example: "No missed deadlines. No portal-hopping. No compliance stress."
- **"Shouldn't" framing**: States how work should feel. Example: "Navigating your HR platform shouldn't feel like a challenge."
- **Light and playful**: Pop culture, football, wordplay, a little emoji. Example: "Sent off. No VAR. No appeals."; "You shouldn't be in a LDR with your salary."
- **Two-sided benefit**: Business and employees win together. Example: "Better for your people. Better for your business."
- **Warm sign-off**: Blue heart 💙 on most posts. Example: most posts.
- **Clear CTA**: "Book a demo" plus link on most posts. Example: "Book a demo 👉 http://bit.ly/PaidHR_Rqd"
- **Spelling**: British (organisations, organisational, analysed, prioritising). Source: posts.

All examples are PaidHR's own words.
