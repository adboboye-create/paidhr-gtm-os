## Summary

- **Target audience (given)**: Nigerian companies, 20 to 500 employees (core 50 to 500), paying staff in Nigeria and remitting PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF monthly. Vuzo / embedded partners excluded. Source: icp.md (scope decisions 2026-11-11).
- **Geography update 2026-11-12**: icp.md v3 adds Kenya and Ghana as expansion markets. All 23 ideas below were built for Nigeria and use Nigerian proof; none is cleared for Kenya or Ghana. Kenya and Ghana ideas get written after icp.md open question 10 is answered. W3 (idea 6) already includes them behind a send gate.
- **Core value proposition**: one HR platform that runs payroll and files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF automatically, so HR leads and founders get their time back and staff get paid right, on time. Source: positioning.md; co:S1.
- **Key differentiators**: Nigerian compliance depth (₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension remitted in 2025, "zero defaults & delays", co:S1, S2); simplicity (cp:K10, sp:V1); reachable support (sp:V4, V5, V10); same-day start with no upfront onboarding fee (co:S7); cross-border payroll in 45+ currencies (co:S1). Not differentiators: WhatsApp, EWA, AI assistant (SeamlessHR has them, cp:K1, K2).
- **Timing note**: today is 12 Nov 2026. The first full year under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 closes on 31 Dec, and annual PAYE returns are due 31 Jan (ss:E1). December and January are the strongest send window of the year for a compliance message. Ideas tagged "Year-end layer" should launch by early December.

### Patterns identified

- **Most common industries on camera**: fintech and digital (Cleva, Mono, Rise, MyCoverGenius, Go54); one multi-site consumer business (Filmhouse); one field-engineering firm (Sygnite). Source: sp:V1 to V10.
- **Company sizes**: almost 20 (Cleva) to 50 to 70 (Go54) where stated; average customer ~62 to ~75 employees (derived). Source: sp:V6, V7; cu: Company sizes.
- **Roles that bought**: Head of HR / People / HR Ops as champion; CEO or founder as buyer or initiator; CFO as checker. Source: personas.md.
- **Common problems**: Excel payroll that stopped scaling; statutory remittance done by hand or by a third party with no proof; a failing provider; payslip admin. Source: sp:V3 to V8.
- **Switch moments named on camera**: growth ("As we grew it just didn't scale", sp:V7), contractors moving to full time (sp:V7), provider failure (sp:V5), CEO telling HR to look (sp:V6), new year onboarding (sp:V10).

### Targeting recommendation (challenging the given ICP)

- **Office-based is the proven core, not frontline.** PaidHR names logistics, retail and manufacturing as main sectors (cu:C3), but every customer on camera with a stated size is office-based. Lead with S2 (office-based 50 to 500) and run frontline (S1) as a measured test. Source: icp.md open question 1; signal-matrix.md.
- **The 20 to 49 band works.** Cleva ("almost 20") is one of the clearest stories, and the Standard plan starts at 20. Keep founder-run teams (S3) in scope as Tier 2. Source: sp:V7; co:S6.
- **There is a channel the ICP doc does not cover**: PaidHR's homepage mentions consultants and agencies running payroll businesses on PaidHR (co:S1), and Cleva's previous setup was an accounting firm filing PAYE by hand (sp:V7). Accounting firms and HR consultancies that run payroll for SMEs are a possible reseller or referral audience (idea 20). Needs PaidHR to confirm a partner offer exists.

## No-AI campaigns

### "31 January" (Year-end, base list)

- **Why it works without AI**: the date and the law apply to every Nigerian employer, so tight targeting (base list, HR lead or founder) does the work. Source: ss:E1.
- **Core message**: three lines. "Annual PAYE returns for 2026, the first year under the Nigeria Tax Act, are due 31 January. PaidHR files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF every month with a receipt for each. Want to see what that looks like for {{company}}?" Confirm the Tax Act support with PaidHR before sending (co:S13 is third-party).

### "Who files your NSITF?" (S3 founders)

- **Why it works without AI**: founders of 20 to 49 person companies often own all four statutory filings themselves (sp:V1, V7), and NSITF is the one most likely to be forgotten (INFERRED, test it). A single pointed question earns replies.
- **Core message**: "Quick one: who at {{company}} files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF each month, and how do you confirm they landed? PaidHR does all four automatically, with a receipt for each. Worth a look?" Mirrors the objection handler for free tools in competitors.md.

## Front-end offer suggestions

1. **PaidRight salary benchmark for their roles**: PaidHR already publishes PaidRight, salary data from 25,000+ workers and 400+ employers (median monthly gross ₦217,000, Jul 2026 snapshot, co:S3, S9). Offer a cut for the prospect's sector or roles. Low commitment, unique data no competitor can copy, and it starts a conversation with HR leads about pay. Confirm PaidHR can produce cuts by sector or role, and the minimum sample size per cut.
2. **Tax Act PAYE check**: take 3 sample salaries from the prospect (or anonymised bands), show PAYE under the 2026 bands and what their four monthly remittances should be. Fits ideas 2, 6 and the "31 January" email. Shows the compliance depth before any demo. Confirm with PaidHR that someone can run it, and the legal wording (not tax advice).
3. **Run one month in parallel, same day**: no upfront onboarding fee and payroll can run on the day of sign-up (co:S7). Invite them to run next month's payroll on PaidHR alongside their spreadsheet and compare. The self-serve quick win: the prospect sees payslips, remittance receipts and approvals on their own data. Trial or pilot terms are unknown; confirm with PaidHR.
