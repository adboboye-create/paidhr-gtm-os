## 1. At a glance

Eight companies, from a 20-person fintech to a cinema group with shift-based staff, describe the same starting point: payroll in Excel, tax and pension worked out by hand, remittances that were slow or did not reflect, and HR people spending their days on admin.

After moving to PaidHR, they describe the same outcome: **payroll in a few clicks, statutory remittance that runs on time, employees who serve themselves, and time back for the people who used to do it all by hand.**

- **"About one hour"**: of manual payments per payroll run, now saved. Source: FAMASI.
- **"About 50 or more"**: payslips no longer handed out by hand each month. Source: Go54.
- **"Three to four months"**: late pension remittances under a previous provider; now monthly and complete. Source: Rise employee.
- **Separate HR and CFO checks**: on every payroll, no longer needed. Source: Go54.
- **"One of the fastest"**: salary disbursement platforms the Filmhouse HR lead has used. Source: Filmhouse.

> "You don't really know what you're suffering until you move to something better."
> Chima, Head of HR, Go54

## 3. The problem they shared

### Payroll lived in spreadsheets

Every company that described its old process started in Excel.

> "As you add on more people it starts to get very complicated and cumbersome. So I had this gigantic Excel spreadsheet where I was tracking everything."
> Tolu Alabi, CEO, Cleva

> "Prior to [PaidHR], we were using the manual computation... the Excel sheet, so... there's a chance of inputting a wrong figure."
> Chima, Head of HR, Go54

Spreadsheets meant mistakes. At Go54, some deductions were not reflected and had to be paid as balances later.

### Statutory remittance was slow, manual and risky

Tax and pension remittance came up more than any other pain.

- **Multi-state PAYE**: "The remittance to the various institutions across the country, it's state by state, is also complex." Cleva's accounting firm had someone "literally walking to go to the tax office to remit." Source: Cleva.
- **Payments that did not land**: "We may pay, it may not reflect at the statutory bodies." Source: Go54.
- **Manual confirmation**: "For remittances I had to do them individually and also go and confirm that the payments have been processed." Source: FAMASI.
- **Compliance as a constant worry**: Mono called compliance and statutory obligations "a huge bone of contention for us." Source: Mono.

### Paying people took hours of hand work

> "I had to individually text everyone on the team to confirm that they have received their payment. I had to [earmark] about one hour of the day whenever I [had] to run payroll just to manually make payments."
> Adeola Ayoola, CEO, FAMASI

### HR was buried in admin

- **Go54**: Head of HR handed out payslips to "about 50 or more people every month."
- **MyCoverGenius**: Head of People prepared payslips "on Excel."
- **Sygnite**: COO found onboarding new staff details "pretty overwhelming" at the start of every year.
- **Cleva**: tracked hire dates, anniversaries and birthdays in a spreadsheet.

### Checks on checks

Because nobody fully trusted the numbers, senior people checked them twice.

> "I have to do my checks, the CFO has to do his check too... Both of us have to do separate checks to be sure that everything is as it used to be."
> Chima, Head of HR, Go54

### The old provider stopped working

Filmhouse Group's previous payroll administrator "had internal issues at that time," and the team needed a replacement that fit the budget and kept month-end running smoothly.

### Payroll runs on the day, in a few clicks

> "It's significantly easier to run payroll at the click of a button. Sometimes I really run payroll on the day... I don't have to prep for it months ahead, no, weeks ahead, no, days ahead. It's just on the day I'm like, 'Oh, today's 28th. Let's go.'"
> Tijesunmi Adewale, Head of People and Culture, MyCoverGenius

> "When the day comes on the 25th it automatically computes what we need to pay them and it just works."
> Tolu Alabi, CEO, Cleva

FAMASI gets a notification before payroll date asking if it is ready to run.

### Statutory remittance is automated and on time

- **Cleva**: PAYE and pension remittance is "automated," replacing the accounting firm's trips to tax offices.
- **Go54**: no longer computes "the taxes and the pension" by hand.
- **Mono**: "Knowing that our employees are paid on time, they are paid accurately, and they are also doing what they are supposed to do in terms of obligations just as citizens of the country... has made it so great for me."
- **Cleva pension**: Cleva can finally offer a pension: "Me and my co-founder have always wanted to do pension for our employees... knowing that [PaidHR] can help us has been really helpful."

> "Without [PaidHR], growing the company and adding more people and either not paying taxes or doing that manually would lead to certain issues, and I'm glad we've been able to avoid that."
> Tolu Alabi, CEO, Cleva

### Salaries arrive fast, with proof

> "It's one of the fastest platforms where disbursement of salaries is concerned, having used quite a few even before here."
> Oluka, Lead HR Operations, Filmhouse Group

Filmhouse also points to:

## 6. Customer stories

### 6.1 Go54: from Excel and double checks to payroll in a few clicks

**Go54** (formerly WhoGoHost) sells digital tools to businesses: domain registration, web and email hosting, Google Workspace setup, SMS and email marketing, and an AI website builder. It has **between 50 and 70 employees**. Chima, Head of HR, is "in charge of everyone."

**Before.** Payroll ran on an Excel sheet. Wrong figures could slip in, some deductions were missed and had to be paid as balances later, and statutory payments sometimes did not reflect at the statutory bodies. Chima and the CFO each checked every payroll separately. Chima also handed out payslips to "about 50 or more people every month."

**Choosing PaidHR.** Go54 was comparing HR systems when its CEO asked Chima to look at PaidHR. After meetings and demos, the team signed up in January (year TBD). Three things decided it: salaries paid "with a few clicks," no more computing "the taxes and the pension" by hand, and performance evaluation in the same platform. Chima also liked the interface on both app and web.

- **After: automation**: Payroll and statutory remittance are automated. The HR and CFO double check is gone: "It's clear. It's clean."
- **After: payslips**: Payslip requests stopped: "Nobody even comes to me."
- **After: EWA**: Staff use Earned Wage Access and "didn't need to go borrowing somewhere else."
- **After: performance**: Performance ratings now need metrics: "It's no longer subjective."
- **After: time**: Chima has time "to focus on other core HR aspects."

> "I feel relieved. And satisfied... You don't really know what you're suffering until you move to something better."

- **Buying committee lesson**: CEO initiated, Head of HR evaluated and runs it, CFO benefits from no longer double checking.

### 6.2 Filmhouse Group: a fast, safe switch when the old provider failed

**Filmhouse Group** runs cinemas. Oluka leads HR operations.

**Before.** The group's previous payroll administrator "had internal issues at that time." Filmhouse needed a replacement that fit its budget and kept month-end payroll running smoothly, with as little room as possible for human input errors.

**Choosing PaidHR.** The demo showed "a very detailed system where you could onboard, offboard, put in absences." Approval workflows stood out: they walk payroll "through different levels of checking, vetting, and you tend to catch the last-minute details that could really affect the entire payment or salary for the month." So did the people: "On the call you could see the dedication, you could see the passion to try to ensure that you understood the entire system even before coming on board."

- **After about 6 to 7 months**: "It has been seamless."
- **Disbursement speed**: Salary disbursement is "one of the fastest" Oluka has used, "having used quite a few."
- **Receipts**: Receipts can be self-generated when an employee queries a payment.
- **Failed payments**: Failed payments are flagged and can be resent at once; account details can be updated after payout.
- **Support**: An account manager is "always readily available," and the FAQ walks users "through step by step."

> "I'll recommend [it] to anyone looking to have that kind of operations where payroll is concerned... and you don't have people complaining about a delay in payment of salaries."

- **Why this story matters**: Filmhouse is the closest match in this set to PaidHR's Tier 1 customer: a multi-site business with shift-based staff ([context/icp/icp.md](../../context/icp/icp.md)). It is also the only switching story told from the buyer's side.

### 6.3 Mono: compliance handled, stress removed

**Mono** builds open banking APIs and no-code tools that let African businesses access financial data, verify identities and collect bank payments. It was recently acquired by Flutterwave. Freda Okusu (Head of People) and Praise Onyonoro (Senior People Associate) run people operations.

**The challenge.** Mono's people team believes "people operations cannot be reactive. It has to be proactive," especially "through our compliance and statutory obligations." Compliance was "a huge bone of contention."

**Why PaidHR.** "One of the pain points that drew me to [PaidHR], not just as a platform but as an organization, was how much they pay attention to compliance and statutory obligations."

- **After: critical processes**: "[PaidHR] has taken over some of the most critical processes that we have and has done it effectively."
- **After: stress**: "Just the fact that they can effectively do this takes a chunk of our stress away."
- **After: pay and compliance**: Staff are paid on time and accurately, and statutory obligations are met.
- **After: modules**: Mono uses the HR and payroll tools and plans to adopt Performance Management.
- **After: account manager**: The account manager fixes issues before being chased: "It's not the regular vendor account manager relationship."

> "[PaidHR], they've really come through for us."

### 6.4 Cleva: from a gigantic spreadsheet to automated multi-state remittance

**Cleva** helps Africans receive international payments and pay online abroad with a virtual card. Co-founder and CEO Tolu Alabi ran payroll personally. The team had grown to "almost 20."

**Before.** Cleva started with one contractor paid by bank transfer. As the team grew, Tolu tracked everything in "this gigantic Excel spreadsheet." Moving contractors to full-time staff brought PAYE, where "the whole computation is not straightforward" and remittance is "state by state." An accounting firm handled remittance, with someone "literally walking to go to the tax office." "As we grew it just didn't scale."

**Choosing PaidHR.** Tolu found PaidHR through its CEO's posts on Twitter, sent a DM and booked a demo. What decided it: "The dashboard looked good, it looked helpful, it was much better than my Excel spreadsheet. The whole tax [and] pension remittance was automated, and lastly they actually seemed like helpful people." A visible founder also gave confidence that support would be there if something went wrong.

- **After about 4 months: remittance**: PAYE and pension remittance are automated.
- **After: onboarding**: New hires get a notification and enter their own details.
- **After: HR records**: Hire dates, anniversaries and birthdays moved off Excel.
- **After: pension**: Cleva is setting up pension for staff, something the founders "have always wanted to do."

> "I feel relief when I log in, especially when I'm adding new people... when the day comes on the 25th it automatically computes what we need to pay them and it just works."

**Choosing PaidHR.** Other HR tools "just didn't seem very simple to use." PaidHR "looked very simple."

- **After: time**: That hour is back: "I have that one hour to be able to focus on other things while being rest assured that [PaidHR] has my people paid."

## 7. The employee's view

Statutory remittance is usually sold to HR and finance. Blessing Ojo, a front-end engineer at **Rise** (wealth management) for over two years, shows what it means for the people being paid.

**Before**, under a different platform: "This month['s] pension can come in like at the end of [the] next three to four months... and I didn't always get emails and notification on my tax payment."

- **With PaidHR: reliability**: "The remittance is very reliable and I'm very much aware of what's going on. I always get email notifications when my tax payment is made."
- **With PaidHR: completeness**: "It has always come in complete... there's a particular timing in the month that the payment is done and it's always accurate and it's consistent."
- **With PaidHR: receipts**: Tax receipts are easy to produce when asked.
- **With PaidHR: no chasing**: "I don't have to be reporting or telling my lead that okay, I'm having this issue."
- **Why it matters for buyers**: reliable remittance builds employee trust and removes a source of complaints that would otherwise land on HR.
